## Auto Summary

Sidney Dekker's The Field Guide to Understanding 'Human Error' is a practical safety-science and human-factors guide, published in its third edition by CRC Press in 2014, with later ebook distribution listing 2017. Dekker argues against treating accidents as the result of careless individuals, replacing the “bad apple” view with a systems approach that examines context, trade-offs, hindsight bias, organizational pressures, and how frontline workers normally create safety. It is aimed at investigators, safety practitioners, designers, managers, and students working in complex sociotechnical systems.
