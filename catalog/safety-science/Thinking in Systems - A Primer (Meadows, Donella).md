## Auto Summary

Thinking in Systems: A Primer is Donella H. Meadows's accessible introduction to systems thinking, edited posthumously by Diana Wright and published by Chelsea Green in 2008, with an Earthscan UK edition in 2009. Based on a 1993 manuscript that circulated informally after Meadows's work on The Limits to Growth, it explains stocks, flows, feedback loops, delays, system traps, and leverage points as tools for understanding complex social, ecological, economic, and organizational problems.
