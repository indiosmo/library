Harvard University selected XVIVO to develop an animation that would take their cellular biology students on a journey through the microscopic world of a cell, illustrating mechanisms that allow a white blood cell to sense its surroundings and respond to an external stimulus. This award winning piece was the first topic in a series of animations XVIVO is creating for Harvard's educational website BioVisions at Harvard.

## Auto Summary

Made in 2006 with Harvard biologists Robert Lue and Alain Viel and XVIVO animator John Liebler, the film helped define the cinematic style of modern molecular visualization after its SIGGRAPH Computer Animation Festival debut. Its best-known sequence is kinesin hauling a vesicle along a microtubule, a stylized but data-informed depiction of intracellular transport within the broader leukocyte-extravasation pathway.
