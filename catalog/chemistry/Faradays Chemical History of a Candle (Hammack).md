Michael Faraday’s The Chemical History of a Candle: With Guides to the Lectures, Teaching Guides & Student Activities is Bill Hammack and Don DeCoste’s 2016 Articulate Noise Books edition and teaching companion to Faraday’s nineteenth-century Royal Institution candle lectures. It preserves Faraday’s popular exposition while adding modern background, lecture guides, and student activities for general readers and science teachers. The work uses the ordinary candle flame to introduce combustion, gases, heat, capillary action, atmospheric chemistry, and the analogy between burning and respiration as an accessible entry into scientific reasoning.
