Gödel, Escher, Bach: An Eternal Golden Braid is Douglas R. Hofstadter's Pulitzer Prize- and National Book Award-winning 1979 interdisciplinary work, later reissued by Basic Books in a 20th-anniversary edition. It interweaves Kurt Gödel's incompleteness theorem, M. C. Escher's visual paradoxes, and J. S. Bach's contrapuntal music through essays, dialogues, puzzles, and formal systems. Its core argument is that self-reference, recursion, and "strange loops" help explain how meaning, intelligence, and consciousness can emerge from symbolic systems.
