## Auto Summary

Judith Swan is Associate Director for Writing in Science and Engineering at Princeton University, holds a biochemistry Ph.D. from MIT, and co-authored with George Gopen the classic 1990 American Scientist article 'The Science of Scientific Writing,' whose reader-expectation approach (stress position, subject-verb proximity, old-before-new information flow) underlies this workshop. The seminar was hosted around 2013 by Public Communication for Researchers (PCR), a graduate-student organization at Carnegie Mellon founded to help researchers communicate with non-specialist audiences. Her central claim goes beyond style tips: readers interpret prose according to structural expectations, so revising structure changes not just clarity but the perceived substance of the science itself.
