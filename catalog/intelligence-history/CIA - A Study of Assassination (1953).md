## Auto Summary

A Study of Assassination is an unsigned Central Intelligence Agency training manual found in the files of Operation PBSUCCESS, created around late 1953 or January 1954 and released with declassified Guatemala coup records in May 1997. It defines assassination, discusses its employment and justification, and lays out planning and techniques for political killing in clandestine operations. The document matters chiefly as a primary source on U.S. Cold War covert action, especially CIA planning around the overthrow of Guatemala's elected president Jacobo Arbenz.
