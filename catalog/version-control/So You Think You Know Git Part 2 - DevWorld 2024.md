## Auto Summary

Scott Chacon is co-founder of GitHub and GitButler and author of the Pro Git book; he originally retired his advanced-Git talks assuming everyone had caught up, then returned to the speaking circuit and found otherwise. Part 1 debuted at FOSDEM 2024 and crossed 500k YouTube views, which prompted him to write this entirely new Part 2 for DevWorld 2024 rather than repeat himself. The talk is a rapid-fire "shotgun" of lesser-known plumbing and porcelain: switch/restore, hooks, gitattributes with smudge/clean filters (RCS keywords, dates, LFS), fixup commits, rebasing stacks with update-refs, Microsoft's Scalar for scaling large repos, and worktrees, closing on his own stacked-branch tool GitButler. He also turned the material into a written "Git Winter Wrapup" blog series.
