## Auto Summary

Stick and Rudder: An Explanation of the Art of Flying is Wolfgang Langewiesche's classic 1944 manual on piloting, published by Whittlesey House/McGraw-Hill, with an appendix on air dangers by Leighton Collins. Developed from Langewiesche's early-1940s Air Facts articles, it explains what pilots actually do with wings, controls, angle of attack, turns, glides, approaches, and landings. Its core aim is to replace misleading ground-based instincts with a practical mental model of flight, making it a durable reference for student pilots, instructors, and experienced aviators.
