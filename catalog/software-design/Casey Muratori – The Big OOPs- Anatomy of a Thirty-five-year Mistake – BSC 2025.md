Casey Muratori is a game and systems programmer known for the Handmade Hero series and the Computer Enhance course; this is his keynote at the inaugural Better Software Conference (BSC) 2025. The talk is heavily research-driven, drawing on hundreds of pages of original sources to trace OOP's intellectual history back through Bjarne Stroustrup's C++ and Alan Kay's message-passing Smalltalk. His argument is narrow rather than a blanket condemnation: the 'mistake' is the habit of drawing encapsulation boundaries around compile-time hierarchies that mirror real-world entities. He frames it through game-development history, contrasting that rigid style with Looking Glass Studios' early entity-component approach (as in Thief: The Dark Project), where systems act on entities instead of objects encapsulating their own behavior.
