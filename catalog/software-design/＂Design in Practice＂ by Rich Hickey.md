## Auto Summary

Rich Hickey delivered this as the closing keynote of Clojure/conj 2023 in Durham, North Carolina, the first Conj after a multi-year pandemic hiatus. Where his earlier talks (notably "Hammock Driven Development") championed thinking time but left the practice of design underspecified, this talk deliberately demystifies it as a concrete, learnable activity rather than something "magic." He frames design as six incremental phases (describe the current state, diagnose problems, delimit scope, decide a direction, design the solution, develop it), measures progress by growth in understanding and recorded decisions, and stresses writing things down with four guides: consistency, specificity, explanation, and succinctness.
