## Auto Summary

Rich Hickey is the creator of the Clojure programming language and the Datomic database; this was his closing keynote at the inaugural Clojure/conj, the first community conference for Clojure. The talk lays out his methodology for solving hard problems away from the keyboard: deliberately engaging the slow, deliberate "waking mind" to load a problem fully, then handing it off to the unconscious "background mind" during sleep or rest (the hammock being the literal venue for thinking, not napping). It has become a widely cited reference in software culture for valuing analysis, design, and reflection over reflexive coding, and pairs thematically with his better-known "Simple Made Easy."
