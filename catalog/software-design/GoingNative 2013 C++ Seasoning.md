Sean Parent's landmark GoingNative 2013 talk, delivered at Microsoft's C++ conference while he was a principal scientist on Adobe Photoshop. It popularized the maxims "no raw loops," "no raw synchronization primitives," and "no raw pointers," arguing that most hand-written loops can be replaced by named standard-library algorithms; the celebrated demonstration rebuilds operations like gather and slide on top of std::rotate, std::stable_partition, and std::find.
